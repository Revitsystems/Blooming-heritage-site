About the School

Educating the whole child, since 2013.

Blooming Heritage School was founded on a simple vision: quality education for young children. Today our community in Lokogoma, Abuja, serves families from Early Years to Secondary, on a blended Nigerian–British curriculum.

We pair academic excellence with character, moral instruction, creativity and leadership, so every pupil becomes capable, confident and creative, ready for the 21st century.
